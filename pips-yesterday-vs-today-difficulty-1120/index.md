# Pips Yesterday vs Today: Why Nov 19 Is Lighter Than Nov 18

If you finished the hard board on November 18 and then felt the one on November 19 was shorter, you were right. The two days use the same Easy and Medium sizes but very different Hard boards.

## The numbers

- [Pips answer for Nov 18, 2026](https://puzzles.newgamescout.com/answers/2026-11-18/): Easy 5 dominoes, Medium 7, Hard 16
- [Pips answer for Nov 19, 2026](https://puzzles.newgamescout.com/answers/2026-11-19/): Easy 5 dominoes, Medium 7, Hard 12

That is four fewer dominoes on the hard board, a 25 percent cut. Fewer dominoes means fewer cells to fill and fewer ways for a mistake to hide.

## What changed in the regions

The Nov 18 hard board is the bigger puzzle with 2 equal, 13 sum, 5 greater-than, 1 less-than regions. The Nov 19 hard board has 6 sum, 1 not-equal, 1 equal, 3 greater-than regions.

## How often does this happen

Across the 459 puzzles in the archive, the most common hard-board size is 16 dominoes (90 times) and 15 follows with 95 boards. A 12-domino hard board is rarer: it appears 70 times.

## What to take from it

1. A smaller board is not always an easier one, since constraints matter more than size.
2. A 16-domino board is the most common size, so budget more time for it.
3. Use [pips answers today](https://puzzles.newgamescout.com/) and [yesterday's Pips answer](https://puzzles.newgamescout.com/yesterday/) side by side if you want to compare solving styles.
