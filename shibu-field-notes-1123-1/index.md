# A Field Guide to Shibuya Roblox Wiki

People looking up Shibuya Roblox clans, cursed techniques and codes usually want the same thing: a page that gives the answer without making them dig. A working checklist, organised by the question you are actually trying to answer rather than by the site's own menu. The pages below are all on Shibuya Roblox Wiki and were picked because each one answers a different part of that question. None of them require an account, and none of them bury the useful part below a wall of text. If you only read one section, read the second one - that is the page most visitors are actually looking for. The rest are there for when the simple answer is not enough and you want to check the detail yourself.

## A good starting point

The [shibuya trello and discord](https://shibuyawiki.wiki/discord-and-trello) page is the place to start when the question is concrete: it puts the https: discord and trello material on one screen instead of splitting it across a long list of posts. What makes it useful is the format. The clan pages are short and factual, which is exactly what you want when you are comparing two of them before spending yen. It is the sort of page you can scan in under a minute, then go back to when you need the exact figure or wording rather than a rough memory of it. A word of caution: the game gets frequent updates; a technique or clan that was strong last month may have been rebalanced. Treat the page as a working reference and re-open it when you need it, rather than trusting a note you made last week. That habit matters more here than it does on a static reference site. If this is the first page you have opened on Shibuya Roblox Wiki, follow one of the links at the bottom before you leave. The site is built out of lots of small pages like this one, and the useful ones are rarely the homepage.
