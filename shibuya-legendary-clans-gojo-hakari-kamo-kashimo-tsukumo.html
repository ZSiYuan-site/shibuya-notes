Shibuya Legendary clans: Gojo, Hakari, Kamo, Kashimo, Tsukumo

Legendary clans in Shibuya are rolled at 1% for the tier, which is four times easier than Mythic. Five clans share it. The Shibuya clan list has a page for each, and this post sums up what each page says.

The odds

The creation screen prints 1% for Legendary. Using the formula 1 minus 0.99 to the power n, you need about 69 rerolls for even odds of any Legendary clan and about 230 for a 90% chance. The reroll odds calculator has a preset for it.

Gojo

shibuya.wiki says the Gojo clan has a 3% chance to awaken Six Eyes, and that you keep it even if you reroll away. That is one fan site, so the page marks it as player reported.

Hakari

The Hakari clan is the one with the biggest documented health bonus: shibuya.wiki says +75 HP. For a beginner who dies a lot, that is the number that matters, especially because a careless death costs more as you grow.

Kamo, Kashimo and Tsukumo

For Kamo, Kashimo and Tsukumo the wiki only has the creation screen entry. It says Legendary clan, 1%, and that nobody has seen the bonus yet. We would rather say that than invent perks. The pages are marked checked for rarity only.

A look at the rare tier

The Rare clan tier is 10%, and one example, Sugawara, gives +5 health. That is small next to Hakari's +75, and it shows why rarity alone does not tell you the value. On the races and clans page we suggest picking by bonus, not by label.

How the sources compare

Everything about perks in this post comes from shibuya.wiki and BloxRant. The percentages come from the screen. If you find a clan bonus in game that contradicts this, use the contact page on the Shibuya Roblox wiki and we will update the page and its checked date.

A practical plan: redeem the working codes first, then check the calculator to see how many rerolls you still need, and decide whether you want Hakari's health or Gojo's Six Eyes chance. If neither appeals, keep the character you have and spend rerolls on the technique instead.

Rerolls are cheaper to come by than most players expect once you count codes, the creation screen purchases and later events. Still, do the arithmetic before you start. At 230 rerolls for a 90% shot, a Legendary clan is a realistic goal for a patient player, while a Mythic clan at 920 rerolls is closer to a long-term project.
