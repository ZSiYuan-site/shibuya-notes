# Two CPSC Product Recalls in Tools and Outdoor Gear This Week

Two notices published on November 12, 2026 are worth checking if you own power equipment. Both come from the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission and are summarised on Recall Radar, a site that rewrites the public notice in plain language. One concerns a small electric pressure washer and the other a dual-fuel portable generator. The two have different hazards and different remedies, which makes them a useful pair to compare.

## QIOMALA pressure washers

The [QIOMALA pressure washer recall](https://recalls.resuhive.com/recall/qiomala-pressure-washers-27084/) covers about 80 units of model HWT12P, sold in green with black and in blue with black. The 1.2 GPM electric washer was sold on Amazon from April through June 2026 for about 130 dollars. The stated hazard is an inadequate power cord, which encourages the use of an extension cord and creates a serious risk of shock and electrocution. No injuries were reported. The remedy is a full refund: stop using it, unplug it, cut the cord in half and send a photo showing the model number.

## Cummins portable generators

The [Cummins portable generator recall](https://recalls.resuhive.com/recall/portable-generators-27087/) covers about 2,960 Onan P5000iDF EFI dual-fuel units, dark gray, producing between 4,500 and 5,000 watts. They were sold at Camping World, Factory Pure, Northern Tool and AP Electric, and online, from May 2025 through August 2026 for 1,640 to 1,930 dollars. Valves were installed incorrectly, so fuel vapour could not vent and pressure built up. The firm received eleven reports of fuel discharging or the cap ejecting, including one splashed in the face. The remedy is a free repair at an authorised service location.

## How to check your own gear

Compare the product name, model number and date range with the label on your unit, usually on the back, bottom or packaging. If it matches, stop using it and follow the remedy in the notice. Both pages include the recall number and the exact source notice. For a wider sweep, the [tools and outdoor category](https://recalls.resuhive.com/category/tools-outdoor/) collects every notice in that group, and the [electric shock hazard page](https://recalls.resuhive.com/hazard/electric-shock/) gathers products recalled for that specific risk. The [CPSC product recalls this week](https://recalls.resuhive.com/this-week/) page is the quickest daily check.

A note on scope: this site summarises public notices and the official notice is authoritative. The pages are updated as new recalls are published.
