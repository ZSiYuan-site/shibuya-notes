Shibuya Mythic clans: Itadori, Okkotsu and Zenin

Mythic is the top clan tier in Shibuya, and at 0.25% a roll it is rare. Three clans share it: Itadori, Okkotsu and Zenin. The Shibuya clans list has one page each. This post compares what is known, and what is not.

The odds

The creation screen prints 0.25% next to the Mythic tier. That is per tier, not per clan. With three clans in the tier, you should expect each one to be a share of that 0.25%. The reroll odds calculator treats it as the whole tier: about 277 rerolls for even odds of any Mythic, and about 920 for 90%.

Itadori

shibuya.wiki says Itadori's Black Flash no longer knocks back and can reset cooldowns. That is the only description available, from one fan site, so the Itadori page marks it as player reported. Black Flash is a signature move from the anime the game is inspired by.

Okkotsu

shibuya.wiki says the Okkotsu clan unlocks Rika with your cursed technique and has an innate speed boost. It also gives advice on rerolling: if you want Rika, chase Okkotsu with clan rerolls, because technique rerolls cannot produce it. Again that is reported from one fan site, not seen on screen.

Zenin

For Zenin the wiki says plainly that it is a Mythic clan on the creation screen and that its bonus has not been seen. The page is marked checked for the rarity only. If you roll it, note what the bonus says and report it.

How to choose

Pick a clan for its bonus, not its rarity. Mythic means rarer, not necessarily better for your build. The races and clans page suggests checking that your race covers whatever your clan leaves weak, and that some clan powers need extra steps before they work. For a technique-heavy character the Incarnated Sorcerer race at 7% adds +10% cursed technique damage according to shibuya.wiki.

Plan your rerolls

Codes help but only a little: the two working codes, PUBLICTESTING and RANKEDFIXES, give 10 clan rerolls between them from one source or another, and you need hundreds. If you are not sure you want to chase a Mythic, a Legendary clan at 1% is much easier to hit. See the sister post about Gojo, Hakari, Kamo, Kashimo and Tsukumo, and the Shibuya wiki home for the full map of pages.

If you want to see how the tiers stack up against each other, the races page lists the race side of the same roll. The race and the clan are rolled separately, which is why people often keep a race they like and spend all their clan rerolls on the tier they want. That split is also why the calculator lets you work out the two odds on their own instead of multiplying them together.
